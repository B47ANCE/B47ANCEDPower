[h1]B47ANCE'd Power[/h1]

Electrical generation, distribution, storage, consumption, and powered-device infrastructure for theWorks ecosystem.

B47ANCE'd Power currently provides the portable-power foundation used by other Works modules. It replaces vague interchangeable battery percentages with reusable battery definitions, persistent energy state, device power profiles, authoritative servicing, and shared energy APIs.

[h2]Current Features[/h2]

[list]
[*]AAA, AA, C, D, 9V, rechargeable-pack, coin-cell, and vanilla-compatible battery profiles
[*]Persistent absolute charge with capacity-aware device consumption
[*]Device profiles that determine compatible cells, capacity, installation rules, tools, and service timing
[*]Timed transactional battery insertion, extraction, and replacement with completion-time revalidation
[*]Replacement timing that combines removal and installation with a configurable kept-open service bonus
[*]Atomic multi-cell servicing with reservations, rollback, and uncertain-operation recovery
[*]Charge-preserving taped battery packs and explicit external-pack connectors
[*]Learnable improvised AA adapters made from an AAA battery and paperclip
[*]Improvised cells that preserve source energy while trading efficiency for electrical resistance
[*]Vanilla battery loot conversion and migration of batteries already installed in supported devices
[*]Mounted weapon-part battery servicing with stable parent-weapon and attachment reconstruction
[*]Digital watches powered by coin cells, mechanical classic watches, and rare solar military watches
[/list]

[h2]Consumption & Energy APIs[/h2]

[list]
[*]Device, installed-load, global, UV, and dynamic consumer multipliers composed into one demand rate
[*]Average duty-cycle accounting for lightWorks strobe, pulse, beacon, SOS, alternating, and emergency patterns
[*]Server-authoritative energy reserve, commit, and rollback operations for cross-mod consumers
[*]Exact charge read-back verification and persistent recovery markers for uncertain energy mutations
[*]Public APIs for battery definitions, powered devices, loads, charge state, consumption, and energy transactions
[*]Bounded source, storage, load, and network provider registries with normalized snapshots and region-scoped discovery
[*]Read-only vanilla generator source/network observations without duplicating or mutating fuel, condition, activation, or native load state
[*]Optional, persistent environmental-event ingress with bounded regional discovery, replay protection, consequence proposals, and fail-closed handler isolation
[/list]

[h2]Ecosystem Integration[/h2]

[list]
[*]Owns electrical generation, storage, distribution, consumption, and infrastructure for theWorks ecosystem
[*]Provides required portable-power services to lightWorks and its registered portable and weapon-mounted lights
[*]Allows Project Nightfall lighting to inherit the same power behavior through lightWorks
[*]Provides optional transactional energy to waterWorks pumps and powered treatment equipment
[*]Accepts optional weatherWorks events without requiring weatherWorks or taking ownership of weather simulation
[*]Allows compatible mods to register batteries, devices, loads, and dynamic demand providers through the public API
[/list]

[h2]Release Scope[/h2]

The current release focuses on batteries, portable devices, energy transactions, and the systems required by lightWorks and Project Nightfall. Full electrical-grid infrastructure is planned for later releases and is not represented as complete gameplay.

[h2]Dependencies[/h2]

B47ANCE'd Power has no required mod dependencies.

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]

Other mods can register battery types, powered devices, installed loads, and demand providers through the public API. Unsupported or failed providers are isolated so they cannot break normal device processing.

[i]Dedicated-server multiplayer compatibility has not yet been certified.[/i]
