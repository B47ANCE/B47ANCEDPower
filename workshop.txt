description=[h1]B47ANCE'd Power[/h1]
description=
description=Electrical generation, distribution, storage, consumption, and powered-device infrastructure for theWorks ecosystem.
description=
description=B47ANCE'd Power currently provides the portable-power foundation used by other Works modules. It replaces vague interchangeable battery percentages with reusable battery definitions, persistent energy state, device power profiles, authoritative servicing, and shared energy APIs.
description=
description=[h2]Current Features[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]AAA, AA, C, D, 9V, rechargeable-pack, coin-cell, and vanilla-compatible battery profiles
description=[*]Persistent absolute charge with capacity-aware device consumption
description=[*]Device profiles that determine compatible cells, capacity, installation rules, tools, and service timing
description=[*]Timed transactional battery insertion, extraction, and replacement with completion-time revalidation
description=[*]Replacement timing that combines removal and installation with a configurable kept-open service bonus
description=[*]Atomic multi-cell servicing with reservations, rollback, and uncertain-operation recovery
description=[*]Charge-preserving taped battery packs and explicit external-pack connectors
description=[*]Learnable improvised AA adapters made from an AAA battery and paperclip
description=[*]Improvised cells that preserve source energy while trading efficiency for electrical resistance
description=[*]Vanilla battery loot conversion and migration of batteries already installed in supported devices
description=[*]Mounted weapon-part battery servicing with stable parent-weapon and attachment reconstruction
description=[*]Digital watches powered by coin cells, mechanical classic watches, and rare solar military watches
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Consumption & Energy APIs[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Device, installed-load, global, UV, and dynamic consumer multipliers composed into one demand rate
description=[*]Average duty-cycle accounting for lightWorks strobe, pulse, beacon, SOS, alternating, and emergency patterns
description=[*]Server-authoritative energy reserve, commit, and rollback operations for cross-mod consumers
description=[*]Exact charge read-back verification and persistent recovery markers for uncertain energy mutations
description=[*]Public APIs for battery definitions, powered devices, loads, charge state, consumption, and energy transactions
description=[*]Bounded source, storage, load, and network provider registries with normalized snapshots and region-scoped discovery
description=[*]Read-only vanilla generator source/network observations without duplicating or mutating fuel, condition, activation, or native load state
description=[*]Optional, persistent environmental-event ingress with bounded regional discovery, replay protection, consequence proposals, and fail-closed handler isolation
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Ecosystem Integration[/h2]
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Owns electrical generation, storage, distribution, consumption, and infrastructure for theWorks ecosystem
description=[*]Provides required portable-power services to lightWorks and its registered portable and weapon-mounted lights
description=[*]Allows Project Nightfall lighting to inherit the same power behavior through lightWorks
description=[*]Provides optional transactional energy to waterWorks pumps and powered treatment equipment
description=[*]Accepts optional weatherWorks events without requiring weatherWorks or taking ownership of weather simulation
description=[*]Allows compatible mods to register batteries, devices, loads, and dynamic demand providers through the public API
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Release Scope[/h2]
description=
description=The current release focuses on batteries, portable devices, energy transactions, and the systems required by lightWorks and Project Nightfall. Full electrical-grid infrastructure is planned for later releases and is not represented as complete gameplay.
description=
